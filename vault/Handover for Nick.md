# Handover for Nick

This is for you, Nick. It explains how to run the PoGo TXK site day to day. You do not need to
know anything about code. If a step here does not match what you see on the screen, stop and
ask Justin; that is a sign something changed, not that you did something wrong.

## What the site is, and what you can change

PoGo TXK is the community's home for Pokémon GO in Texarkana. Visitors see the news, the
meetups, the map of stops and gyms, photos, and live raid information. You can change four
things from the admin area:

- **News posts**: the articles on the news page.
- **Meetups**: the dates, times and places of community get-togethers.
- **The map**: the stops, gyms and other places, with their photos and credits.
- **The photo library**: the pictures, their descriptions and who gets credit for them.

You cannot break the raid, egg and research pages. Those fill themselves in from another site.

## Signing in

Your admin account is separate from Discord. It does **not** use your Discord login, and Discord
cannot sign you in to it.

1. Go to the admin sign-in page. Justin will give you the exact address; save it as a bookmark.
2. Type your **username** or your **recovery email**. Either works in the same box.
3. Type your password and press Sign in.

Use a password manager for the password. It is long on purpose.

## If it says you are locked out

Five wrong passwords in a row lock the sign-in for a while. It is a safety feature, and it
protects you as much as anyone.

- The lock lifts by itself. The first time it is **1 minute**, then **5 minutes**, then **30
  minutes**, and after that **1 hour at most**. It never locks for longer than an hour.
- The message tells you how long is left. Wait that long before trying again.
- While you are locked, **even the correct password is refused**. That is normal. Typing it
  again and again does not help; waiting does.
- Locking you out does not lock out the other admin. Each account has its own lock.

If you are locked and do not want to wait, you can use the reset link below instead.

## If you forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Lost the password?**
2. Enter your recovery email and send the request.
3. Open the email. Click the link in it.
4. Choose a new password (at least 16 characters) and type it twice.

Things to know:

- The link works **once** and lasts **30 minutes**. If you click it twice or wait too long, ask
  for a new one.
- Resetting **signs you out on every other device**. That is on purpose: if someone else had
  your old password, they are out too.
- A reset also clears any lockout.
- The page always says the same thing whether or not your email is on file. That is on purpose,
  so strangers cannot use it to find out who the admins are.
- **If no email arrives, nothing on the page will tell you why.** That is also on purpose.
  Check your spam folder, wait a few minutes, and then ask Justin.

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in as long as you keep using the site. Each time you use it, the two weeks start
again. If you do not use the admin area for two weeks, you will be asked to sign in again. That
is normal.

If you ever get a message saying you were signed out while you were writing something, sign in
again (the message has a link) and your work is kept in the page where possible. Still, for a
long post, copy the text somewhere safe before you save.

## Writing a news post

Every post has a **status**:

- **Draft**: only admins can see it. Use this while you are writing.
- **Scheduled**: it has a date and time in the future. It goes live **by itself** at that time.
  You do not need to be signed in.
- **Published**: everyone can see it.
- **Archived**: taken down from the public pages but kept, so you can bring it back.

Posts are written in a simple style called Markdown. Two lines are enough to start:

- Put `#` and a space before a heading, and `**` on both sides of a word to make it bold.
- Put `[words you click](https://example.com)` to make a link.

Use the preview to check how it will look before you publish.

## Meetups

- Times are always **Texarkana time** (Central). You do not need to convert anything.
- If a meetup is **cancelled**, it stays on the site and is shown as cancelled, so nobody drives
  to an empty park. Do not delete a meetup to cancel it.
- A meetup can have an **RSVP / Campfire link**. It must be a normal web address that starts
  with `https://`. Anything else is refused.

## The map

- **Click** an empty spot on the map to place a new location, then give it a name straight away.
- **Drag** a pin to move it, and check the place on the map afterwards.
- Do not delete a location you only want to hide. **Archive** it instead. It stays in the system
  and you can bring it back.
- **Always credit photos.** Every photo needs the right credit and a link back to where it came
  from. Never remove a credit. The Texarkana Gazette photo especially has a byline that must
  stay.

## "Also announce to Discord"

Posts and meetups have a box called **Also announce to Discord**. Ticking it sends **one**
message into the community's channel with a link back to the site.

- It **cannot be taken back**. Once the message is in Discord, the site cannot remove it.
- A **draft** waits. Nothing is sent until the post is actually published.
- A **scheduled** post is announced shortly after it goes live, usually the next time somebody
  opens the home page. It will not be on the dot.
- A **cancelled** meetup is never announced.
- Untick the box if you are only fixing a typo.

## Deleting things

Posts and meetups are **archived**, not destroyed. Archiving takes them off the public pages but
keeps everything, so a mistake can be undone. **Only Justin can delete something for good.** If
you truly need something gone, ask him.

## What not to touch

On the main admin page there is a panel that **copies content from the old website**. It was
used once to move everything over. **Leave it alone.** It is not part of your work.

## When something looks wrong

Check these before anything else:

1. **Is it the real address?** The site has older preview addresses that start with a string of
   letters and numbers. Those are frozen copies and never update. Use the main address.
2. **Did you wait?** After you change something, give it about 90 seconds before you decide it
   did not work. The site keeps a short-term copy of its pages.
3. **Try a refresh**, and then try a private browser window.
4. **Still wrong?** Write down what you did, what you expected, and what happened (a screenshot
   helps), and send it to Justin. Please do not try to fix it by making more changes.

## Who to call

**Justin.** For a missing reset email, a lockout that will not clear, a deletion, or anything
that looks broken.
